HOW TO PLAY

🎮 Movement & Controls

W A S D — Move your character

Left Click — Use the currently selected skill toward mouse cursor

Right Click — Cycle active skill: Skill 1 → 2 → 3 → 1 …

⚔️ Skills (order set before each game)

Ranged Attack — Fires a glowing projectile toward the cursor.
Damage: 1 000  |  Cooldown: 2 s

Melee Attack — Strikes enemies in a cone toward the cursor (short range).
Damage: 2 000  |  Cooldown: 2 s

Shield — Wraps you in a golden barrier.
50 % damage reduction  |  Duration: 3 s  |  Cooldown: 5 s (after expiry)

👾 Enemies

Horror demons constantly chase you — there are always 5 on the map.

Each demon has 5 000 HP and respawns after 5 s.

Contact damage: 3 000 every 3 s  |  Ranged spit: 500 every 3 s

🏆 Objective

Find and step onto the golden exit tile on the right edge of the dungeon.

A stone wall blocks the direct path — navigate around it!

Your HP: 10 000

💡 Tips

Use Shield before entering a group of demons.

Melee deals double the damage of Ranged — get close when it's safe.

Killed demons respawn, so don't waste time hunting — head for the exit!

ORDER YOUR SKILLS

Drag the cards to set the order.
Skill slot 1 is your starting active skill.
